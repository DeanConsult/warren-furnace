Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions About HVAC Service in Warren MI

Do you offer 24-hour emergency furnace or AC repair in Warren?

Yes. We understand that heating and cooling problems do not just occur during normal business hours. We offer evening and weekend appointments as well as 24-hour emergency service, and will make every effort to get to your home or business as quickly as we can.

Do you offer financing for a new furnace or AC?

Yes. We understand it can be difficult to afford the upfront cost of a new furnace or air conditioner. For your convenience, we offer a financing program so you can pay over time.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Warren MI and most of the Metro Detroit market for both residential and commercial heating and cooling needs.

Do you service all brands and models?

Yes. Our technicians service all makes and models of furnaces and air conditioners. We have relationships with the major manufacturers who provide us with discounted pricing on new units, which we pass on to you.

How do I know if I need furnace repair or a full replacement?

The average lifespan of a furnace is 15–20 years. If you have a furnace that is up in age and also requires extensive and expensive repairs, it may be the right time to invest in a new one. Our technician will evaluate and provide a written quote so you can decide with no pressure.

How often should I have my furnace and AC serviced?

We recommend having your furnace and air conditioner inspected and cleaned at least every year or two. Routine servicing can nearly double your unit’s lifespan while saving you on future repairs.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Warren Furnace LLC is fully licensed and insured in Michigan. Most of our staff attend continued education on a quarterly basis to stay up to date on best practices in the industry.
